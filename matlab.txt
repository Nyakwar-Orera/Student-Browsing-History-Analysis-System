In the Sentinel enforcement system, multiple strict safeguards and precautions are active to prevent accidental or unfair account disablement.

1. Are Bookmarks Considered Policy Violations?

No. Bookmarks are 100% exempt from policy violations and strikes.

How it works: Before any blocklist, category rule, or strike evaluation runs, the engine checks the student's Chrome bookmarks (_is_bookmarked_by_student()).
Result: If the domain is saved in the student's browser bookmarks, Sentinel treats it as an authorized academic study shortcut:
Decision: action: allow
Status: account_status: active
Strikes added: 0 (completely bypassed).
2. How Instagram & Social Media are Handled (Login vs Transient Redirects)

The system distinguishes between an accidental redirect / landing page and deliberate active use:

Unauthenticated / Login Gateways (/login, /accounts/login, popups, ads):

If a student hits Instagram via an ad click, search redirect, or popup landing page with brief time (< 30s and hit_count <= 2), Sentinel classifies it as an Ad / Transient Landing.
Zero Strikes: Sentinel displays:

"📌 Ad / Redirect Notice: 'instagram.com' was opened via advertisement or popup redirect. The ad should be closed instantly! No violation penalty has been recorded."

No strike is recorded, and the account remains active.

When Does it Count as a Violation?

Only when there is proof of deliberate active engagement:
The student is logged in and browsing past the login barrier (feeds, direct messages, profile pages, reels), OR
Sustained active dwell time (dwell_sec >= 20 seconds or repeated hits hit_count >= 3).
3. Precautions Before a Student Account Can Be Automatically Disabled

For any junior student (Darajah 1–7) to be disabled, the following safeguards are enforced:

5-Strike Progressive Discipline (No Immediate Disable for Social Media or Movies):

Social media (Instagram, Facebook, etc.) and non-educational YouTube movies never trigger an immediate lockout.
A student must accumulate 5 deliberate strikes before an automatic suspension is triggered (max_warnings_before_block: 5).
Strikes 1 through 4 only display advisory warnings to give the student opportunity to close the tab.

Immediate Lockout Is Strictly Limited to 5 Extreme Categories:

Immediate suspension without 5 warnings is restricted to high-severity network threats:
adult content
proxy_vpn (bypass tools)
torrent (P2P file sharing)
video_chat (unmoderated chat like Omegle/OmeTV)
anonymous_qa
Everyday browsing, social media landings, ads, and streaming do not qualify for immediate lockout.

Fresh Start Date Cutoff (October 31, 2026):

Browsing records created before October 31, 2026 are excluded. Re-enabled students cannot be penalized for pre-existing history.

Cleared History on Re-Enablement:

Whenever a student account is reinstated (via Sentinel or Active Directory), their previous strike tally is cleared so they start with zero active strikes.
